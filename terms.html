Porchlight Mix is run by Iain, based in Washington State, USA.

What you get

Porchlight Mix provides digital mix-feedback reports for musicians. A paid report is an automated mix-feedback report — $4.99 per report, delivered digitally.

Free re-check

Each paid full report includes one free re-check. During early access, free tester reports also include one re-check, with the same rules. Early access ends when Porchlight Mix launches. After that, re-checks come only with paid reports. Here's how it works:

  • One per report. Each report comes with one re-check. Each report on the Songwriter Plan comes with its own re-check, and re-checks don't count toward your 10 reports a month.
  • Same song. The re-check is for a revised mix or master of the song in your report. A different song needs its own report.
  • Within 14 days. Send the revised version within 14 days of the date we email your report. We'll tell you how to send it when your report arrives.
  • What you get. We run the same checks with the same reference set. You get a short pass or fix result for each fix in your report, plus anything new that needs attention. It isn't a new full report.
  • Timing. Re-check results usually arrive within 2–3 days.
  • Your file. The revised file is handled like any upload: deleted within 7 days of upload, never public, never shared, and never used to train AI.
  • Refunds. Using your re-check doesn't change your right to a refund under our refund policy.
  • A re-check you don't use has no cash value.

Guidance only

Reports are provided as-is, as guidance. They are notes to help you decide what to change. They are not a promise of a particular release result.

Audio you upload

You must own the audio you upload, or have the rights to upload it so we can prepare your report.

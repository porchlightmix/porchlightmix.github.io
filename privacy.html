Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Porchlight Mix is operated by Iain Kennedy, Langley, Washington, USA. This page says what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your email address
  • The music genre you tell us
  • When the product launches, the audio you upload so we can prepare your report

The waitlist form is hosted by Tally. When you submit it, Tally receives your email and the music genre you choose.

The free Release Check

The free checker at checker.html analyzes your audio file entirely in your web browser. The file isn't uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we don't receive or store your audio or its results.

How we use it

We use that information only to deliver your mix-feedback reports and to send updates you asked for. We do not sell your information.

Your audio

Uploaded audio is deleted after we deliver your report, within 30 days.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your card numbers.

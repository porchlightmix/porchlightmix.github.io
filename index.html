Most mix checkers were built for EDM.

Most AI mix tools compare your song to dance-music targets: big sub-bass, hyped highs, loudness pushed to the limit. Run a fingerpicked ballad or a worship set through one and the advice can work against the song.

Genre-blind advice

"Low end is 6 dB below target. Boost sub-bass. Increase loudness to −8 LUFS."

Following that on an acoustic track leaves you with boom, a squashed vocal, and a song that sounds worse after Spotify turns it down.

Advice that understands your song

"Your vocal drops about 3 dB under the strummed guitar in the choruses. Try a little vocal ride or a gentle 2–4 kHz dip on the guitar."

Notes compared against songs like yours, in words you can act on in any DAW.

How it works

No plugins, no jargon, no waiting days for a stranger's opinion.

1

Upload your mix

Drop in a WAV, AIFF, FLAC or MP3 and pick your style: singer-songwriter, folk, country, band or worship. Add a reference track if you like.

2

Get your report in minutes

We measure loudness, true peak, clipping, frequency balance, stereo width and how the vocal sits, then explain it all in plain English.

1. Peaks are too hot for streamingYour true peak is −0.1 dBTP. Streaming services recommend −1 dBTP or lower. Pull your limiter ceiling down to −1.0 dB so the song doesn't distort when it's converted.
2. The vocal gets buried in the choruses1:02–1:34, 2:20–2:52
The lead vocal drops about 3 dB against the strummed guitar. Ride the vocal up 1–2 dB in those sections, or carve a gentle 2–4 kHz dip in the guitar.
3. A little boxy around 300 HzLow mids are fuller than typical for this style. A wide 2 dB cut around 250–350 Hz on the guitar bus should open things up.
Working wellDynamics breathe nicely for an acoustic song, and the mix holds up in mono.

Example data for illustration only. Spec references: Spotify recommends about −14 LUFS integrated and a true peak at or below −1 dBTP.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser. Export a mix from any DAW (or even a phone recording app) and upload it.

Is a human listening to my song?

No. Your report is generated automatically from measurements of your audio and written up in plain English. Your song is private, and the file is deleted after analysis.

Will you use my music to train AI?

No. Never.

Does it work for mastered tracks too?

Yes. Upload a mix before mastering or a final master before you send it to your distributor.

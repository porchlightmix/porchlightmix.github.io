About Porchlight Mix

Last updated October 20, 2026

Porchlight Mix makes release-ready mix checks and free release tools for acoustic and vocal music.

Who's behind it

Porchlight Mix is run by Iain, a long-time musician based in Washington State, USA. It's a small independent business, and questions and feedback go straight to him at porchlightmix@gmail.com.

What we make

  • The free Release Check (try it): measures your song's loudness (LUFS), true peak, clipping, silence and file format in your browser, and shows what Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube and Amazon Music will do with it. Your file is never uploaded.
  • The free Loudness Preview (try it): shows how much Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Amazon Music, TIDAL and Deezer will turn your song up or down, and lets you hear it. It uses the same measuring code as the Release Check, and your file is never uploaded.
  • The free Release Checklist (try it): builds a dated, printable to-do list from your release date, distributor and platforms, with links to each company's own rules. Your choices stay in your browser.
  • The Full Report ($4.99 per song, pre-order now): plain-English notes on the mix itself. It starts with your top 3 fixes in priority order, each with a clear action. It compares your song with our own reference recordings in your style, and shows how the lead vocal sits against the band. It includes your Release Checklist dates and one free re-check within 14 days.
  • The Songwriter Plan ($9.99 a month for 10 full reports, each with the same extras): coming at launch.
  • Turnaround: full reports are emailed, usually within 2–3 days.

Who it's for

Independent singer-songwriters, home recordists, folk and country artists, small bands and church worship teams. If you record real voices and real instruments and mix your own songs, it's for you.

Why we built it

A lot of mix advice assumes a loud, bass-heavy pop or EDM sound. On a fingerpicked ballad or a live worship set, that advice can make the song worse. Porchlight Mix compares your mix with reference recordings in your own style instead.

How the check works

  • Loudness is measured with the international standard ITU-R BS.1770, the same method Spotify says it uses. Loudness range follows EBU Tech 3342.
  • Streaming targets come from each service's own help pages where they publish them, like Spotify's loudness guide. Where a service doesn't publish a number, we say so and use independent measurements.
  • Full reports compare your mix with our own reference set for your style: recordings released under Creative Commons licenses that allow commercial use (CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA), each license checked on the track's own page. We chose them; they aren't songs you pick. The comparison covers loudness, punch, tonal balance, stereo width and, when there's a lead vocal, vocal level.
  • Reports are generated automatically from measurements. Your song is never public and is never shared with anyone. It's never sent to an outside AI service, and it's never used to train any AI model. We analyze it with our own software on our own computer (this includes an open-source audio-separation tool that splits the vocal from the instruments). No human listens to it.
  • Uploaded songs are deleted within 7 days of upload.

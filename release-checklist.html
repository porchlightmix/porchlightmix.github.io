Questions about releasing music

How far ahead should I upload my song?

Most distributors suggest about 3–4 weeks. DistroKid suggests about 4 weeks, CD Baby at least 3 (4–6 is ideal), TuneCore 3–4, and UnitedMasters 3. Amuse needs 7–14 days depending on your plan. Earlier gives you time to pitch to Spotify.

When do I pitch my song to Spotify?

At least 7 days before release, in Spotify for Artists. The song must be unreleased and already delivered by your distributor. Pitching on time also puts it in your followers' Release Radar.

Will an acoustic or live version get into Release Radar?

Maybe not. Spotify says it doesn't include some alternative versions of tracks, like acoustic, karaoke or live versions, in Release Radar.

What size should my cover art be?

3000 × 3000 pixels, square, saved as a JPG in RGB color. Every distributor we checked accepts that. Some accept other sizes, so check yours.

Is my checklist saved anywhere?

Only in your own browser, so it's there when you come back on this device. There are no accounts and nothing is sent to us.
